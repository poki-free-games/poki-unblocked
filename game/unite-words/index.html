Unite Words

  • Free
  • No download
  • Best on a computer
  • Word Games
  • Quiz Games

§1About the game

Unite Words is a word puzzle about sorting tiles into groups of four related words. Each set belongs in one row, so the job is to work out which words belong together and move them into place.

A round starts with rows of mixed words. You select a word, swap it with another, and keep rearranging until four related words share a row and the set completes. Some levels hold special sets that reveal new words once finished, which means the board can grow as you play. A timer runs the whole time, and a hint highlights a group if you stall. The thinking is in spotting the connection before the clock forces a guess.

§2How to play

  • Use a mouse or touchpad to select a word and swap it with another between rows.
  • Place four related words in the same row to complete a set.
  • Finish the special sets on some levels to reveal new words to group.
  • Complete every set before the time runs out.
  • Stuck? Use a hint to highlight a group of words.
