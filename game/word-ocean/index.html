Word Ocean

  • Free
  • No download
  • Works on phones
  • Word Games

§1About the game

Word Ocean is a word puzzle game where you build words from a ring of letters. Each word you need to find gets its own row of blank squares, and the rows sit in columns so you can see how many words are left and how long each one is.

The letters wait in a circle beneath the squares. You connect them in order to spell a word, and a correct one fills in its squares. Clear every row to finish the level. The letter count grows the more you play, so the words get longer as you go. Nothing is timed, which leaves room to think about a stubborn row instead of rushing it. It works as a short break between classes: read the squares, trace a few letters, and see what fits.

§2How to play

  • Look at the rows of blank squares: each row is one word, and the square count shows the word's length.
  • Find the circle of letters beneath the squares.
  • Connect letters in order to build a word.
  • Fill every missing word to clear the level.
  • Expect more letters in the circle the more you play.
