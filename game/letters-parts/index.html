Letters Parts

  • Free
  • No download
  • Works on phones
  • Word Games
  • Quiz Games

§1About the game

Letters Parts is an alphabet game built around one job: find the piece that finishes a letter and click it. Each round shows a letter with a gap in it, and you pick the piece that fills the gap.

The play is short and quiet. You look at the shape of the letter, work out which piece belongs in the open space, and click it. Nothing is timed, so you can take a moment to compare shapes before you choose. The game was developed by Nau.kids and runs in a browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

§2How to play

  • Look at the letter and the empty space where a piece is missing.
  • Click the missing piece to complete the letter.
  • Play on a phone, tablet or computer.
