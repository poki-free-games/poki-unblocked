Runner Garden 3D

  • Free
  • No download
  • Works on phones
  • Brain Games

§1About the game

Runner Garden 3D is a 3D running game set on a flower-lined path. Your job is to steer a character down the route, swipe to turn left or right, jump and slide past obstacles, and collect flowers until the basket is full and the level is complete.

A round is a straight run with blocks, woods and barriers in the way. Turning at the wrong moment or missing a jump ends the level, and so does falling off the path, so each stretch asks you to read what is ahead and pick a lane early. The flowers give you a reason to move across the path instead of hugging one side. It is a short-session game for a school break, and the simple tap-to-start, swipe-to-move setup keeps it quick to pick up.

§2How to play

  • Tap or click to start the run.
  • Swipe left or right to turn the character.
  • Swipe to jump and slide under or over obstacles.
  • Collect flowers to fill your basket and finish the level.
  • Hitting an obstacle or falling off the path fails the level.
