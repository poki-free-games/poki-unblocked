Solitaire Daily

  • Free
  • No download
  • Works on phones
  • Board & Card Games

§1About the game

Solitaire Daily is a card game where you sort a shuffled deck into four Ace piles, each running from Ace to King. You build the tableau by placing red cards on black and black cards on red, uncovering the cards underneath as you go.

The rules stay close to standard solitaire. When no move is left on the board, you take a card from the pile to open further possibilities. The difficult part is planning ahead: a card you bury now may be the one you need later, and the Ace piles only accept cards in order. A highscore is tracked, so repeat runs have something to measure against. Each session is a single deal you can work through at your own pace.

§2How to play

  • Place red cards on black cards and black cards on red to build the tableau.
  • Move cards to the Ace piles in order, from Ace to King, to win.
  • Stuck? Take a card from the pile to open further possibilities.
  • Clear the tableau and finish all four Ace piles to complete the game.
