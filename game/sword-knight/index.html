Sword Knight

  • Free
  • No download
  • Works on phones
  • Word Games

§1About the game

Sword Knight is a pixel adventure game about finding the way out. Obstacles and puzzles sit between you and the exit, and you work through them one at a time.

The game spreads its puzzles and obstacles across 3 worlds. You move through each one on the way to the exit, using your sword when something needs attacking. On a computer you move with the WASD keys and swing with the space bar; on a phone you tap to move and use the sword to attack. The challenge comes from reading each obstacle and puzzle before you commit, so a short session still asks you to think before you swing.

§2How to play

  • On a computer, move with W A S D.
  • Press Space to attack with the sword on a computer.
  • On a phone, use touch to move and the sword to attack.
  • Find the way out through 3 worlds of obstacles and puzzles.
