Cars Card Memory

  • Free
  • No download
  • Works on phones
  • Board & Card Games

§1About the game

Cars Card Memory is a card-matching game where you flip cards to reveal Easter items, remember where each one sits, and pair them up. Clear every card on the board to finish the level.

Each level is harder than the one before, and a timer limits how long you have. You click a card to turn it over, then click another to look for its match. Two cards that don't match go back face down, so the board rewards attention more than speed. The name says cars, but the pictures are Easter items, so watch what actually appears rather than what you expect.

It runs in the browser and works on phones. Short rounds make it easy to fit into a break, and the memory work keeps it from feeling like pure luck. The question at the end is how far up the levels your memory can carry you.

§2How to play

  • Click a card to flip it and see the Easter item underneath.
  • Click a second card to try for a pair.
  • Matched pairs stay cleared; unmatched cards flip back down.
  • Clear all the cards on the board to complete the level.
  • Each level is harder than the last, and the clock is limited.
  • Use the mouse to play.
