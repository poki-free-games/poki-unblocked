Wordloft: Balloon Crossword

  • Free
  • No download
  • Best on a computer
  • Word Games

§1About the game

Wordloft: Balloon Crossword is a crossword puzzle where letters float in on balloons. Each crossword has a bunch of balloons hanging below it. Tap the balloon with the letter that fits the highlighted cell, and the letter flies into the grid.

You can tap any cell to fill it out of order. If you tap a wrong letter, it turns red; tap it again to remove it. Fewer mistakes mean more stars. Spend stars on Wind to blow away wrong balloons, or use the light bulb for a hint. There are 100 crosswords in 10 themes: Home, Animals, Journey, Nature, City, Jobs, Sports, Music, Kitchen, and Capitals. Travel across the map in a hot-air balloon. No timer, so you can take your time.

§2How to play

  • Read the clue at the top of the screen.
  • Tap the balloon with the letter that fits the highlighted cell. The letter flies into the crossword.
  • Tap any cell to fill it out of order.
  • Tap a wrong (red) letter to remove it.
  • Use the light bulb for a hint or the Wind to blow away wrong balloons.
