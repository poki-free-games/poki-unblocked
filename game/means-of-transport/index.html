Means of Transport

  • Free
  • No download
  • Works on phones
  • Quiz Games

§1About the game

Means of Transport is an educational game about different ways to get around. You click on the co-responsive means of transport, matching each one to what the game shows.

Nau.kids developed it as a kids-friendly activity for phones, tablets, and computers. The goal is to learn transport types by clicking the right one. There are no timers or scores to worry about, so you can take your time and click through the set at your own pace.

§2How to play

  • Click on the co-responsive means of transport to match it.
  • Play on a phone, tablet, or computer.
  • No keyboard controls are needed.
