Crossword Puzzles AWS-PROD

  • Free
  • No download
  • Works on phones
  • Word Games

§1About the game

Crossword Puzzles gives you a grid and a list of clues. Choose a vertical or horizontal clue, and the cells that answer belongs in light up in a different color. Click those cells and type your word.

A correct answer turns the region green, so you can see your progress fill the board. When every cell is green, the puzzle is complete and you can ask for a new one. Wrong answers leave the cells as they are, so you can try again without losing the grid.

The work is in matching each clue to a word that also fits the letters you have already placed. Crossing answers check each other, so one shaky guess can stall several clues at once. It rewards patience and a decent vocabulary more than speed, which makes it a good fit for a quiet break between classes.

§2How to play

  • Pick a vertical or horizontal clue from the clue list.
  • The matching cells on the board are marked with a different color.
  • Click a marked cell and type your answer.
  • A correct answer turns that region green.
  • Fill every cell with green to complete the puzzle, then ask for a new one.
