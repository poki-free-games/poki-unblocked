ArrowXis Brain Escape Puzzle

  • Free
  • No download
  • Best on a computer
  • Brain Games

§1About the game

ArrowXis Brain Escape Puzzle is a logic puzzle about removing arrow pieces in the right order. Each piece has a tail, and you can only take it off when that tail is free. Tap a free piece and it disappears, which can open up the pieces around it.

The order you pick matters. In some challenge modes, matching colors or directions clear automatically, and a good move sets off a chain reaction that clears several pieces at once. There is no timer, so you can study the board and plan before you tap. Hundreds of handcrafted levels start out gentle and get harder as you go, and the three challenge modes change which pieces disappear when you make a move.

§2How to play

  • Tap an ArrowXis piece whose tail is free to remove it.
  • Clearing a piece can free the tails of pieces next to it.
  • Depending on the challenge mode, matching colors, matching directions, or all free pieces may disappear automatically.
  • Plan your taps to set off chain reactions and clear the whole puzzle.
