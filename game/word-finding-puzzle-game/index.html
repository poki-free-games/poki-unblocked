Word Finding Puzzle Game

  • Free
  • No download
  • Works on phones
  • Word Games

§1About the game

Word Finding Puzzle Game is a word search: a grid of letters hides words you have to find. Swipe across the letters to mark a word, and keep going until the grid is cleared.

Each round is a hunt. Words run horizontally, vertically or diagonally, and they can read forwards or backwards, so a line of letters you skim past can be the one you need. You swipe the letters in order to claim the word, then move on to the next. Finding them all takes patience and a habit of reading the grid in both directions. The game is built to increase your vocabulary and exercise your brain, and the searching itself is calm work rather than a race.

§2How to play

  • Swipe the letters of a word to select it.
  • Words can run horizontally, vertically or diagonally.
  • Words can read forwards or backwards.
  • Find every hidden word in the grid to finish.
