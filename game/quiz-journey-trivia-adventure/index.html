Quiz Journey: Trivia Adventure

  • Free
  • No download
  • Best on a computer
  • Quiz Games

§1About the game

Quiz Journey: Trivia Adventure is a general knowledge quiz built around a level map. Each question you answer moves you along the map, and correct answers earn stars and coins that go toward building a collection piece by piece, with rare items to hunt down.

When a question stumps you, four hints are available: 50/50 removes two wrong answers, Ask the Audience shows what others picked, Skip moves you past the question, and Shield protects you. Daily free rewards and a lucky wheel give you another reason to return. Tapping the question text skips the typing animation, so you can read and answer at your own pace.

§2How to play

  • Click or tap an answer to select it.
  • Click or tap the question text to skip the typing animation.
  • Click or tap a hint button to use one: 50/50, Ask the Audience, Skip or Shield.
  • Answer questions correctly to earn stars and coins and move along the level map.
  • Collect items as you progress and hunt for the rarest ones.
  • Return daily for free rewards and to spin the lucky wheel.
