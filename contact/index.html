Contact poki

You can reach the poki team by email at contact@poki-unblocked.onrender.com. Write to that address directly; it is plain text here, so type it into your mail app rather than clicking.

Email is the only way to reach us. There is no phone support, and messages sent to other channels will not get a reply.

What to write about

  • A broken game: tell us the game name and what happens, such as a blank screen, a button that does nothing, or a score that will not save. A screenshot helps.
  • A game suggestion: name the game or describe the kind you want, like a quick word game or a two-player board game for a school break.
  • A rights question: developers and rights holders can ask about a game on the site, including removal requests.

Expect a reply in about a week. We read every message, but a fix or an answer can take longer than the first reply.
