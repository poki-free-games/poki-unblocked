Brain games here are short puzzles and memory tests you can pick up and drop in a few minutes.

Brain games ask you to hold a shape in your head, spot a pattern, or remember where something was. Some are about planning moves ahead, like Falling Blocks, where you rotate and slot pieces before the stack reaches the top. Others test speed and attention: Word Swipe has you drag across letters to find words hidden in a grid, and Get the Stars - Extended asks you to collect every star in a level without getting stuck.

If you want a memory test, try Dont Forget, which leans on remembering what you saw. ArrowXis Brain Escape Puzzle is the one for careful route planning, and Runner Garden 3D mixes quick decisions with collecting. Start with Word Swipe for a quiet minute, then move to Falling Blocks when you want something that keeps your hands busy.
