Cookies and browser storage on poki

This page explains what poki stores in your browser, what the games we embed may store, and how to block or delete it. The short version: our own pages set no tracking cookies.

What poki itself uses

The site keeps only the browser storage a game needs to work: your best score, a saved level, sound on or off, and similar settings. That data stays in your browser and is not used to follow you around the web. Clear your browser data and it is gone.

Embedded games and ads

Games are served inside a frame on our pages. The company that made a game, and the ad partner that runs ads in that frame, may set cookies or use similar storage inside it. Those cookies belong to them, not to poki, and we cannot read or change them from our side. If a game frame shows an ad, the ad partner may use cookies to limit repeats or measure how an ad performed.

How consent works

Starting a game counts as your consent to the cookies that game and its ad partner set inside the frame. If you would rather not accept them, do not start the game. Browsing the game lists and reading pages like this one does not load those frames.

Blocking and deleting cookies

  • Open your browser settings and find the privacy or cookies section.
  • Block third-party cookies to stop most cookies set inside game frames.
  • Delete existing cookies and site data to remove saved scores and settings.
  • Use a private or incognito window to start each session clean.

Blocking cookies can break a game's save feature or stop an ad from loading, and some games may not start at all. That is a fair trade if you only want a quick round and do not care about keeping a score.
