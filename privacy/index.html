Privacy at poki

poki is a free browser-games site with no accounts, no sign-up and no forms. You can play without telling us who you are, and we do not collect personal data on the site itself.

What we do not collect

There is nothing to register for and nothing to fill in. We do not ask for your name, email, age or school, and we do not build profiles of visitors. We do not sell data, and there is no data to sell from the site pages.

Server logs

Like most websites, our server keeps standard technical logs when a page is requested. These include the IP address, browser and device type, the time of the request and the page asked for. We use them to keep the site running and to spot abuse or attacks, and we keep them for a limited time only. They are not used to identify individual players.

Games from other developers

The games on poki are embedded from independent developers and from a game distribution partner. When a game loads, it runs inside a frame on the page, and that frame is controlled by the developer or partner, not by us. Those frames may show ads and may use cookies or similar storage in your browser under their own privacy policies. We do not control what they store, and we cannot read it. If you want to know exactly what a particular game does, its own policy is the place to check.

Children

poki is made for quick breaks and thinking games, and many players are students. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, write to us and we will delete it.

Clearing cookies

You can remove cookies and similar storage at any time from your browser settings. In most browsers, open Settings or Preferences, find the privacy or site-data section, and clear cookies and site data for this site or for everything. You can also block third-party cookies there, though some embedded games may then stop working or forget your progress within a game. Private or incognito windows drop this storage when you close the window.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page. Questions about privacy can go to contact@poki-unblocked.onrender.com, and we will answer as clearly as we can.
